About SHYA HealthCare

SHYA HealthCare stands for S → Self-Care, H → Healing, Y → Youthful, A → Affection.

We are dedicated to making healthcare simple, intelligent, and accessible. Using **AI-driven prediction models** and **personalized recommendations**, we guide users towards better health, disease prevention, and a balanced lifestyle.

Our Mission

  • Provide accurate disease predictions using modern machine learning techniques.
  • Promote healthy lifestyles with personalized diet, workout, and precaution tips.
  • Make healthcare knowledge accessible to everyone, anytime, anywhere.
  • Encourage people to take charge of their own self-care and wellness.
  • Build a youthful & caring community focused on healing and compassion.
